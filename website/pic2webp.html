An offline WebP converter, so your photos never leave your device.

Converter websites make you upload every photo to a server farm before it becomes WebP. Pic2WebP is an offline WebP converter: drag in a folder of images and the conversion happens on your own machine, with the network disconnected.

Where that matters:

  • Personal photo libraries. Years of family photos should not pass through a stranger's queue to save a few megabytes.
  • Client and product imagery covered by NDAs or launch embargoes, convert before anything is public.
  • Bulk website optimization. Recursively convert whole folder trees to WebP locally, with quality presets and a target-size mode, then upload the results straight to your host.
  • Slow or metered connections. Uploading gigabytes to "save bandwidth" defeats the point; local conversion costs nothing.

WebP is supported by every modern browser and most image tools, cutting image weight by 25-50% at the same visual quality, and converting locally is the fastest, most private way to get there.

Available for macOS and Windows. One-time purchase, updates included.

Questions, answered.

What formats can I convert?

JPG, PNG, GIF and more. The output is always WebP, which is supported by every modern browser and most image tools.

Do you upload my images?

Never. Conversion happens entirely on your device. The app works fully offline.

Is it a subscription?

No. One-time purchase of $4.99, updates included, nothing to renew.

Can I convert back to PNG?

The app is deliberately one-directional: the point is a web-ready format. Keep your originals; conversion creates new files and never overwrites the source.
