Before you buy

How many computers can I use?

One code works on up to 3 computers, Windows, macOS or both. If you replace a machine, you release the old one yourself on the licence page.

What happens when the 7-day trial ends?

The app asks for a one-time purchase to keep going, and your activation code unlocks it on the spot. Anything you made during the trial stays on your machine.

Can I get a refund?

Every purchase includes a 7-day money-back window: email us with your order reference and we refund in full, no forms and no questions. For EU and UK statutory rights, the refund policyexplains the window and how to request one.

Are future apps included in the family?

Yes. One payment covers every app that exists today and the ones added later.

Payment and tax

Prices are in US dollars. Where sales tax or VAT applies, we calculate and collect it at checkout. Cards and PayPal both accepted.

Where you buy

Refunds

If a Rocktier app is not right for you, you can ask for a refund. The refund policy explains the window and how to request one.
