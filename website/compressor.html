Questions, answered.

Which file types can it compress?

PDFs, Office documents and images, in batches of any size.

Will I lose quality?

The quality preset you choose controls the trade-off. Pick the one that fits the job before the batch runs.

How does the disk pre-check work?

Before compressing, the app estimates how much disk space the batch can free, so you can decide with the numbers in hand.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Compression happens entirely on your machine, offline.
