Tell us what broke. We will show you what changed.

Rocktier apps have no analytics and no telemetry, so the only way we hear about a problem is when you tell us. This page explains where to write, what we put on the record, and how to check that a fix really shipped.

How to reach us

  • Inside any Rocktier app: open the Help menu and choose Send feedback. It opens a draft email on your device.
  • Or write to hello@rocktier.com, or use the contact page. A short description, the app version and what you expected to happen are enough.
  • Please do not attach private documents. A screenshot with the sensitive parts removed is usually more useful than the file itself.

What we publish, and what we do not

We have no user reviews that we are allowed to publish yet, so this page has no star ratings, no selected praise and no quotes. We will not invent any.

When someone lets us quote them, we will print the words unchanged, the unflattering ones too, next to the version that addressed them. Until then, the honest record is the changelog.

A fix log you can check

These entries are copied from our public changelog, with real version numbers. None of them is presented as a response to a user email: they came from our own checks and from what we found while testing. Follow the link to read the full log.

  • 2026-11-03, Rocktier Markdown 1.0.20: the preview's synced indicator could stay dark even when both panes were in sync. Fixed.
  • 2026-10-24, Rocktier Markdown 1.0.17: Intel Mac builds added, so the app runs on older Macs as well as Apple Silicon.
  • 2026-10-25, Rocktier OCR 0.2.11: an earlier update had removed the interface and the app opened to a blank window. The interface was put back.
  • 2026-11-04, Rocktier Pic2WebP 1.9.20: in the default window the convert button could sit below the visible area. It now stays visible while only the settings scroll.

What happens after you write

  • We try to reproduce it on the same platform. If we cannot, we will write back and ask for one more detail.
  • We fix the cause, not just the symptom, and add a check that would have caught it earlier.
  • The change goes into the public changelog with its version number, so you can verify the claim instead of taking our word for it.
  • We cannot promise every report lands in the next release. We can promise we will not quietly drop it, and that we will tell you if we decide not to do it.
