Your files never leave your device.

One privacy policy for the whole Rocktier family. Every app works on your files locally, and no app connects to the internet unless you ask it to. No accounts, no uploads, no tracking.

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

Scope

This policy applies to every Rocktier app and to rocktier.com. The current family: Rocktier PDF, Rocktier Pic2WebP, Rocktier Markdown, Rocktier OCR, Rocktier CAD Viewer, Rocktier Write, Rocktier Journal and Rocktier Compressor. Each new app that joins is covered by this same policy.

What we collect

Rocktier apps are built so they need no personal information to work. We ask for no name, no email, no phone number, no location, and no account. There is nothing to sign up for, and we run no usage analytics.

The files you compress, convert, edit, merge, or sign are processed on your device and never leave it. We have no servers that receive them and no way to see them.

Our apps contain no telemetry, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party analytics of any kind. If a version ever offers crash reporting, it is off by default: a report is sent only after you approve that specific report, and declining it costs you nothing.

A Rocktier app can make at most two kinds of requests, both described in full below: a license activation, and an update check that also re-checks your licence status. Both happen only when you ask for them: by entering a license code, or by clicking "Check for updates". Neither is needed to use the app, and it works fully offline without them. Some versions distributed through an app store ship without both entry points, because the store handles purchase and updates. A build that offers neither of them makes no network requests at all.

The complete request list

Across the family, these are the only three requests a direct-download build can make. They map to the two kinds described above: activation, and update checking (which may contact both our server and GitHub). Every one of them needs you to act first; none happens at startup or in the background, and none carries file contents, file names or paths.

  • POST https://rocktier.com/api/activate - when you enter a license code. Sends the code, a device fingerprint used for the activation limit, and the OS name.
  • GET https://rocktier.com/api/revalidate?txn=... - when you click "Check for updates" while a receipt exists. Sends only the transaction id already in that receipt.
  • GET https://api.github.com/repos/<repo>/releases/latest - when you click "Check for updates". A public API call with no body, no query parameters and no custom fields; the repository name is part of the URL.

Some app-store builds omit the menu entries that trigger these requests. What is left in a given build is visible in its Help menu, and the two kinds of request described above are the only ones any of them can make.

Update checks

No Rocktier app checks for updates on its own. The app does not connect to the internet when it starts, while you work, or in the background. When you click "Check for updates", the app requests one small file that lists the latest version: and that is the only thing it asks for.

That request is a plain GET to GitHub's public releases API. The app sends no body, no query parameters and no custom fields: the repository name is part of the URL, and the only header the app sets is Accept. Standard request information: such as your IP address and the User-Agent supplied by your system, which may identify your operating system and CPU: is handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy, and we do not receive it. The request never carries your license code, your device identifier, or anything about your files. If a newer version exists, the app tells you and links to the official release page. We never download or install anything without asking, and we do not track which version you run.

Activating a license is a separate request to our own server. Its reply contains only a signed receipt, the product code and your device count: it does not return a version number, and the app never asks for one. On the latest releases, clicking "Check for updates" also re-checks your licence status with our server once: the request carries only the transaction id already in your receipt, and a refunded licence is deactivated locally. A build that ships without both entry points sends nothing to us.

License activation

Activation is the only action that sends an identifier, and it happens only when you choose to activate a license or manage your devices. The app sends your license code, a device identifier, and the name of your operating system, so that we can count activations against the device limit and let you release a machine you no longer use.

We store the license code, the device identifier, and the activation date. We store nothing else, and your documents are never involved.

How your data is processed

All work happens locally on your device using its native capabilities, with no internet needed. Closing an app or deleting it removes its data from your device; nothing is kept anywhere else.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with anyone, for any reason.

When you download an app from a store (Microsoft Store, Apple App Store, or similar), the store operator handles standard transaction and device information under its own privacy policy. That handling is governed by the store operator, not by us, and we receive no personal data from it.

Storage and security

Your data lives only on your device, protected by your operating system's own measures: app sandboxing, file permissions, and encryption at rest. There is no Rocktier cloud, no Rocktier database, and no data center that can be breached.

Children's privacy

Rocktier apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. In practice, there is nothing of yours, or of a child's, for us to hold.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA/CPRA (California), and China's PIPL, you have the right to access, correct, delete, and export your personal data. Because Rocktier collects no personal data, these rights are met by design. For any question, contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

About this website

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising. Your theme preference is stored in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and access time) under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, when a change is significant, note it on this page. It remains a single policy for the whole family, however large it grows.
