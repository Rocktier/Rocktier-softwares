Questions, answered.

Where does my diary live?

In the app's data folder on your own machine, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. There is no cloud copy and no sync.

What protects my entries?

AES-256-GCM encryption, with a key derived from your passphrase through PBKDF2. Without the passphrase, the entries on disk are unreadable.

Can I back up my diary?

Yes. Backup export produces an encrypted ZIP archive you can copy to an external drive or anywhere else you keep backups.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Writing, search and export all run offline, on your machine.
